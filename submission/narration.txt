Humanoid Lift Lab turns Gemini Argon into a robotics engineer. A working warehouse controller can still fail when the payload changes. Here, our reference controller drops an eleven pound tote. We simulate the attempt in MuJoCo, record video and telemetry, and ask Argon to diagnose the failure. It identifies grip and clearance problems, then rewrites the Python controller. The revised program lifts the same payload and holds it. A deterministic scorer checks lift height, hold time, falls, drops, and balance assistance. Across eight development payloads, from eleven to eighty eight pounds, both video and text adaptation improved from seven passes to eight. We retain the best controller to avoid regressions. Inspect every round, or ask questions by voice. This is a modified, assisted simulation. Hardware transfer and held out evaluation remain future work.
