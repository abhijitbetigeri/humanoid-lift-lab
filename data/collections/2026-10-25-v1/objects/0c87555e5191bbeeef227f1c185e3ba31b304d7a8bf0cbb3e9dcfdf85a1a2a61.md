## Diagnosis

- **5 kg to 40 kg (all trials FAIL at 0:00.0 / 0:01.0):**
  - **Timestamp & Telemetry:** At `0:00.0` in phase `1_approach_squat`, every trial immediately raises a `TypeError: FakeRobot.squat() got an unexpected keyword argument 'torso_pitch'` at line 45 (`robot.squat(depth_m=squat_depth, duration_s=squat_dur, torso_pitch=crouch_pitch)`), aborting execution before any controlled grasp or lift can occur.
  - **Physical & Code Cause:** The runtime `robot.squat` implementation only accepts `(depth_m, duration_s)` as arguments (matching the high-level API summary `robot.squat(depth_m, duration_s)`), whereas lines 45, 113, and 124 passed `torso_pitch=...`.
  - **Secondary Physical Observations from the 1.0 s fallback telemetry:** Even during the 1.0 s default simulation window, for masses $\ge 15\text{ kg}$, `com_margin` drops negative at `0:00.5` (`-0.026 m` at 15 kg down to `-0.100 m` at 30–40 kg), causing `assist` to spike up to `200 N` and joint torques (`hip_pitch`, `knee`, `waist/back`, `shoulder`) to saturate (`tau_frac = 1.0` for $m \ge 25\text{ kg}$). This happens because:
    1. Without explicit whole-body posture control via `robot.set_posture(...)` to shift the hips/pelvis backward (via ankle pitch, knee flexion, and hip/waist counter-pitch) and widen/stabilize the stance, the combined CoM shifts forward outside the support polygon as soon as load is taken.
    2. Holding or reaching the box too far forward (`grasp_x` up to `0.36 m` at lines 54, 68, and `retract_x_final` only pulled to `0.18–0.23 m` at line 132) creates an excessive horizontal moment arm $\tau \approx m g d$, saturating the back, hips, and shoulders at $\ge 25\text{ kg}$.

## Changes

1. **Fix `robot.squat` API calls (fixes crash across 5–40 kg):** Call `robot.squat(depth_m, duration_s)` using positional arguments only (`robot.squat(squat_depth, squat_dur)`), removing the unsupported `torso_pitch` keyword argument on lines 45, 113, and 124.
2. **Use `robot.set_posture` with mass-scaled joint targets for torso counter-pitch and CoM compensation (addresses negative `com_margin` and high balance assist at 15–40 kg):** Discover available waist, hip, knee, and ankle pitch joint names dynamically from `robot.joint_names()` and apply smooth `set_posture` adjustments scaled by $\alpha = \text{clamp}((m - 5)/35, 0, 1)$ so the robot shifts its pelvis back via ankle dorsiflexion and leans its trunk slightly backward during the lift and hold.
3. **Pull the box closer to the pelvis/torso scaled by mass (addresses `tau_frac = 1.0` saturation at 25–40 kg):** Scale the horizontal retraction target in the pelvis frame as $x_{\text{retract}}(m) = 0.19 - 0.05\alpha$ (down to `0.14 m` for 40 kg) as soon as the box clears the floor, minimizing the horizontal lever arm on the shoulders, waist, and hips.
4. **Slow down lift trajectories and scale grip preload with mass (addresses peak torque saturation and slip up to 0.017 m at 25–40 kg):** Increase lift duration smoothly with mass ($T_{\text{lift}} = 2.2 + 3.2\alpha\text{ s}$) to reduce inertial acceleration $a$, and apply a mass-scaled normal force $N(m) = \frac{1.95 \, m (g + a_{\text{peak}})}{2 \mu} + 45\text{ N}$ prior to liftoff with continuous slip monitoring during lift and hold.
