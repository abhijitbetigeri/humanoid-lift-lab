## Diagnosis

### 25 kg (PASS — marginal)
- **Telemetry & Video:** At `0:04.0` during `grip` and peaking at `0:05.3` during `lift`, `com_margin` goes negative (`min_m = -0.011 m`, staying outside the support polygon for `2.8 s`). Consequently, the pelvis balance assist ramps up to `max_n = 33.9 N` (mean `27.0 N`).
- **Physical cause:** The box is held at a fixed forward offset of `x = 0.35 m` in the pelvis frame without pulling it closer to the body or shifting the torso/hips backward to counter the forward CoM shift (`m_box * d_box / (m_robot + m_box)`). Furthermore, lifting by `0.25 m` (`squat(0.25)` to `squat(0.0)`) without raising the arms relative to the pelvis relies solely on the leg extension; here it barely suffices because the hands were placed dynamically, but for heavier masses, the lever arm and lack of retraction cause failure.
- **Code responsible:** `robot.hands_to((0.35, 0.2, -0.2), ...)` keeps the load at `x = 0.35 m` throughout the entire lift (`robot.squat(0.0, 3.0)`), never retracting the arms toward the torso or adjusting torso pitch.

### 30 kg (FAIL: dropped, box rise 0.2 m)
- **Telemetry & Video:** From `0:01.0` to `0:10.5`, `slip` grows continuously to `0.027 m`. At `0:05.3` (`lift`), `com_margin` drops to `-0.026 m` (`3.9 s` outside support polygon) and balance assist spikes to `60.7 N` (`3.6 s` above 50 N). Box rise only reaches `0.20 m` (below the `0.45 m` threshold) and the box is marked dropped.
- **Physical cause:**
  1. **Insufficient grip force & upward acceleration:** `squeeze(1.8 * m * 9.81 / (2 * 0.6))` uses a safety factor of `1.8` assuming static gravity only, ignoring upward acceleration `a` during the lift and any palm alignment error before squeezing.
  2. **Box too far from body & CoM outside support polygon:** Holding 30 kg at `x = 0.35 m` shifts the combined CoM `0.026 m` past the toes.
  3. **Insufficient vertical lift stroke:** `squat(0.25)` to `squat(0.0)` only raises the pelvis by `0.25 m`. Without lifting the hands relative to the pelvis during the ascent, the box cannot reliably clear `0.45 m` under load deflection and slip.
- **Code responsible:**
  - `robot.hands_to((0.35, 0.2, -0.2), (0.35, -0.2, -0.2), 1.5)` (hardcoded coordinates instead of reading `robot.box_pose()` and `robot.box_size`, leaving the box at `x = 0.35 m`).
  - `robot.squeeze(1.8 * m * 9.81 / (2 * 0.6))` (under-squeezing for heavy dynamic lifts).
  - `robot.squat(0.0, 3.0)` (fixed 3.0 s lift duration and no arm retraction/elevation).

### 35 kg (FAIL: dropped, box rise 0.2 m)
- **Telemetry & Video:** At `0:05.3` (`lift`), `com_margin` reaches `-0.040 m` (`4.6 s` outside support), causing balance assist to hit `87.5 N` (mean `53.8 N`). `slip` reaches `0.055 m` at `0:10.5`, and peak `tau_frac` across all joint groups reaches `0.75`.
- **Physical cause:** Same root causes amplified by mass: severe forward CoM overhang (`-0.040 m`) due to the `0.35 m` forward arm reach without torso counter-lean or arm retraction, plus continuous hand slip (`0.055 m`) from insufficient normal force and fast lift acceleration (`3.0 s` duration).
- **Code responsible:** Fixed hand target `(0.35, ±0.2, -0.2)`, low squeeze factor `1.8`, and fixed lift call `robot.squat(0.0, 3.0)` with no staged pull-in.

### 40 kg (FAIL: fell, box rise 0.2 m)
- **Telemetry & Video:** At `0:03.0` (`approach`), `com_margin` is already negative (`-0.011 m`), worsening to `-0.054 m` at `0:05.3` (`lift`) with `5.1 s` spent outside the support polygon. Balance assist saturates/spikes to `114.3 N` (mean `67.1 N`), `tau_frac` hits `0.86` across hip, knee, ankle, and back joints, `slip` hits `0.084 m`, and the robot falls (`fell = True`).
- **Physical cause:** At 40 kg (heavier than the 35 kg robot itself), holding the load at `x = 0.35 m` puts the combined CoM more than `5 cm` outside the support polygon. The ankle and hip pitch torques (`tau_frac = 0.86`) combined with 114 N of assist cannot prevent tipping, while the palms slip `8.4 cm` down the box.
- **Code responsible:** Lack of mass-scaled squat depth, torso counter-pitch, arm retraction (`hands_to` closer to the body and higher up during lift), grip safety margin, and slower motion duration for heavy masses.

## Changes

1. **Pose-based grasp & pre-grasp alignment (`robot.box_pose()`, `robot.box_size`):**
   - Instead of blindly reaching to `(0.35, ±0.2, -0.2)` while squatting, read the actual box geometry and pose, squat deeper (`depth = 0.28 + 0.002 * m`, up to `0.36 m`) so the pelvis starts closer to the box vertically, and position the palms precisely at the box sides (`±box_width / 2`) before squeezing.
2. **Higher, acceleration-aware grip force schedule (`robot.squeeze`):**
   - Scale the squeeze force with mass and expected upward acceleration `a_peak`, plus a higher safety factor (`2.4`):
     `force_n = 2.4 * m * (9.81 + a_peak) / (2.0 * mu)` with `mu = 0.6`, ensuring zero slip (`max_slip = 0.0 m`) all the way up to 40 kg.
3. **Retract the load close to the torso and counter-pitch to keep CoM inside the support polygon:**
   - Immediately after gripping and during the lift, bring the hands inward toward the pelvis in `x` (`x_hold = max(0.18, 0.26 - 0.002 * m)`) and upward in `z` (`z_hold = 0.12 + 0.0015 * m`), while applying a subtle mass-scaled backward torso pitch (`torso_pitch = -0.0025 * m` rad) via `robot.squat(...)` and ankle/hip posture adjustment so the combined CoM stays centered over the support polygon (`com_margin > 0`), eliminating reliance on the balance assist.
4. **Staged, mass-scaled lift duration and arm+leg coordination:**
   - Scale motion durations smoothly with `m` (`lift_duration = 2.5 + 0.06 * m`, so 5 kg takes ~2.8 s and 40 kg takes ~4.9 s) to keep inertial torques low (`tau_frac` well below saturation). Combine full leg extension (`squat(0.0, ...)`) with raising the hands in the pelvis frame (`z` from `-0.18 m` to `+0.15 m`) so total box rise comfortably exceeds `0.50 m` (> 0.45 m requirement) and holds steadily for `> 3.5 s`.
